# Billing & Account FAQ

## Billing Questions

### How does billing work?
Acme Platform bills monthly or annually. Annual plans receive a 20% discount. Charges are processed on the first day of your billing cycle. We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and PayPal.

### Can I change my plan mid-cycle?
Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately and you are charged the prorated difference. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing cycle — you keep your current features until then.

### How do I cancel my subscription?
Go to Settings → Billing → Cancel Subscription. Your data is retained for 30 days after cancellation, after which it is permanently deleted. You can export your data at any time before that window closes.

### What happens if my payment fails?
We retry failed payments 3 times over 7 days. If all retries fail, your account is downgraded to the Free plan. Your data is preserved. You can re-enter payment details at any time to restore your plan.

### Do you offer refunds?
We offer a full refund within 14 days of the initial purchase if you are unsatisfied. After 14 days, refunds are handled case-by-case by our billing team. Contact billing@acmeplatform.com.

### How do I get an invoice?
Invoices are automatically emailed to the billing contact after each charge. You can also download all past invoices from Settings → Billing → Invoice History.

## Account Management

### How do I reset my password?
Click "Forgot Password" on the login page and enter your email. You will receive a reset link valid for 1 hour. If you don't receive it, check your spam folder or contact support.

### Can I have multiple accounts with the same email?
No. Each email address can only be associated with one Acme Platform account. However, you can be a member of multiple workspaces under the same account.

### How do I transfer workspace ownership?
Go to Settings → Workspace → Members. Click the three-dot menu next to the new owner's name and select "Transfer Ownership." The current owner will be downgraded to Admin.

### What is two-factor authentication (2FA)?
2FA adds an extra security layer to your account. We support authenticator apps (Google Authenticator, Authy) and SMS-based 2FA. Enterprise plans can enforce 2FA organization-wide via SSO (SAML 2.0).

### How do I delete my account?
Account deletion is permanent and irreversible. To delete: Settings → Account → Delete Account. You must be the workspace owner to delete the workspace. Individual members can leave a workspace from Settings → Account → Leave Workspace.

## Data & Privacy

### Where is my data stored?
Data is stored on AWS in US-East-1 by default. European customers can request EU data residency (Frankfurt, eu-central-1) on Growth and Enterprise plans.

### Is my data backed up?
Yes. We perform automated backups every 6 hours with 30-day retention. Enterprise customers can request custom backup schedules.

### GDPR compliance
Acme Platform is fully GDPR compliant. We act as a data processor and you remain the data controller. A Data Processing Agreement (DPA) is available on request for Enterprise customers.
